Production pipeline

From briefto release.

Six stages, each closed by a review gate, so problems are caught where they are cheapest to fix.

01

Brief & scope

Theme, math and feature set arrive from game design and product. They become an asset list, a schedule and a capacity plan across the team.

Deliverables
Asset list · Milestone plan · Workload split
Review gate
Scope sign-off
Who
Production, game design, product

02

Concept & style

Moodboards, character and key-art concepts, and a style frame that fixes palette, rendering and mood before production starts.

Deliverables
Moodboard · Character concepts · Style frame
Review gate
Art direction review
Who
2D Artists

03

Production art

Symbols, characters, backgrounds and frames painted to the style frame, layered and named so they are ready to rig.

Deliverables
Symbol set · Backgrounds and frames · Layered source files
Review gate
Art review for consistency and mobile readability
Who
2D Artists

04

UI & UX

HUD, paytable, pop-ups and feature flows, laid out mobile-first for portrait and landscape.

Deliverables
Game screens · Paytable · Feature flows
Review gate
UX review on device
Who
UI/UX Designers

05

Animation

Spine rigs, symbol wins, character idles, big-win sequences and transitions, tuned for timing and for the runtime budget.

Deliverables
Spine projects · Win sequences · Transitions
Review gate
Animation review
Who
Spine Animators

06

Integration & launch

Exports and handoff to development, visual QA in the build, then the launch pack: store tiles, banners and promo motion.

Deliverables
Export package · Visual QA pass · Launch pack
Review gate
Release sign-off
Who
Graphic Designers, Motion, development, QA
